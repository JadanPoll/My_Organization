# My Organization

What would it be like to grow up within a community where engineers, scientists, technically curious youth, interesting projects, equipment, and opportunities are continually within reach?

I want to explore how we can create and sustain such a community. A makerspace is one particularly interesting physical nucleus, but the ambition is broader: the diffusion of knowledge, experience, wisdom, and talent across a community and across generations.

This repository is both a place to develop the idea and an accumulating intellectual resource for future investigations. It should remain useful to us and to personal AIs years from now, even as technologies, circumstances, and our own thinking change.

## How this can grow

Gather examples of existing organizations, interviews, project experiences, budgets, institutional failures, useful operating mechanisms, and observations from our own experiments. Preserve the actual sources and original thoughts where useful. Distinguish what we would like to happen from what we have observed happening.

The organization does not need to exist in its final form before we begin learning. A recurring meetup, a single engineer's talk, or a small shared project can teach us something important.

Keep the public lake free of identifying youth information, private contact details, confidential board material, and employer-proprietary information. Personal or sensitive records need a separately controlled location; a public repository is not a private archive.
